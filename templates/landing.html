FAQ

Answers, plainly.

Does it create real Google Forms?

Yes. They appear in your Google Forms like any form you made by hand, and you can edit them there. You need a Google account to sign in.

Can people upload files to my form?

On a hosted form, yes, up to 10 MB per file. Google Forms can’t be created with file-upload questions by other apps, so those forms are hosted here.

Is it free?

You can start without paying. The AI chat runs on a free quota with daily limits, so heavy use can be slowed or paused.

What happens to my data?

Google tokens are stored encrypted, you only see your own forms, and uploaded files are stored privately. Questions in a clear format are read without AI; other requests use Google’s free Gemini API, so don’t paste confidential data. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I change a form after it is live?

Google Forms, yes: open them in Google Forms. Hosted forms can’t be edited after publishing yet, so delete and create a new one.
