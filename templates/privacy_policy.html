Privacy Policy

FormPilot AI

Effective Date: October 22, 2026
Last Updated: October 22, 2026

FormPilot AI ("we", "our", or "us") is a web application available at https://formpilotai.onrender.com/ that helps you create Google Forms and hosted forms by describing them in natural language. This Privacy Policy explains what data we access, how we use it, where it is stored, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it.

1. Information We Collect

Google account information

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture. We use these only to create your account and identify you inside FormPilot AI.

Google user data we access through Google APIs

With your permission, FormPilot AI requests the following Google OAuth scopes:

  • openid, email, profile: to sign you in and identify your account.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/forms.body: to create Google Forms in your Google account and add the questions you asked for.

We use these permissions only to provide the features you request. We do not read your emails, Google Drive files, contacts, calendar, or any other Google data. We do not read responses to your Google Forms.

Content you provide

This includes the prompts and instructions you type in the chat, files you upload so we can find questions in them (PDF, DOCX or TXT, up to 10 MB each), the form drafts generated for you, and the forms you create.

Responses submitted to hosted forms

If you create a hosted form, people who fill it out submit their answers and, where the form asks for it, files (up to 10 MB each) through FormPilot AI. We store these on your behalf so you can view them.

Technical information

Our application and hosting provider record your IP address, browser type, request timestamps, and error logs for security and troubleshooting.

2. How We Use Information

  • To sign you in and keep your session secure.
  • To turn your instructions into form questions.
  • To create Google Forms in your Google account when you confirm.
  • To create and host forms on FormPilot AI, and show you their responses in your dashboard and CSV downloads.
  • To show your generated forms and their links in the app.
  • To prevent abuse, secure the service, and fix technical problems.
  • To respond to your support and deletion requests.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

FormPilot AI's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  • We use Google user data only to provide and improve the user-facing form creation features of FormPilot AI.
  • We do not sell Google user data.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, including retargeting or interest-based ads.
  • We do not use Google user data to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine learning models.
  • We do not allow humans to read your Google user data unless you give explicit consent for specific data, it is necessary for security or abuse investigation, it is required by law, or the data is aggregated and anonymized for internal operations.

4. AI Processing

To generate forms from your instructions, the text you type in the chat (and the text of any question file you upload) may be sent to the Google Gemini API. Questions written in a clear format (such as "Question 1", "A.", "B.") and simple field lists are processed on our own server without AI.

We do not send your Google access tokens, your Google account data, or responses submitted to your forms to the Gemini API.

We use the free tier of the Gemini API. Under Google's terms for the free tier, content submitted to it may be used by Google to improve its products. Please do not enter confidential or sensitive information in the chat. Google's handling of this content is governed by the Gemini API Additional Terms.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Your data is stored on our server and database hosted on Render. We store:

  • Your name, email address, and account identifier.
  • Your Google access tokens, stored encrypted, used only to create Google Forms in your account on your request.
  • Your chat messages, form drafts, and the forms you created.
  • For hosted forms: the responses and files submitted by people who fill out your form. Files are stored privately and only the form owner can download them.

We keep this data while your account is active. When you request deletion, we delete your account data within 30 days, except for limited records we must keep for security or legal reasons. Google Forms already created in your Google account remain in your Google account and are not deleted by us.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information or Google user data. We share data only with:

  • Render, our hosting and database provider.
  • Google, to sign you in, create forms through the Google Forms API, and process AI requests through the Gemini API as described above.
  • Authorities, only when required by law or to protect the security of the service.

7. People Who Fill Out Hosted Forms

If you create a hosted form, the people who submit it provide their answers and files to you through FormPilot AI. You are responsible for how you use that information. We store it only to show it to you in your dashboard and CSV download, and we do not use it for any other purpose.

8. Data Security

We use HTTPS encryption in transit, encrypted storage of Google access tokens, secure session cookies, CSRF protection, rate limiting, file type checks on uploads, and restricted access to our servers and database. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Revoking Google Access

You can remove FormPilot AI's access to your Google Account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/connections. After revoking, we can no longer access your Google Account, but data already stored by us remains until you request deletion.

10. Data Deletion

To delete your account and all data we store about you, email bhaveshkumarjha4@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject "FormPilot AI - Data Deletion Request". Do not include passwords or access tokens. We will confirm and complete deletion within 30 days.

11. Children's Privacy

FormPilot AI is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us and we will delete it.
