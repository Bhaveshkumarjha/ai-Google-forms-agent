Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

Effective Date: October 22, 2026
Last Updated: October 22, 2026

Welcome to FormPilot AI ("we", "our", or "us"). FormPilot AI is a web application designed to help users create Google Forms using natural-language instructions. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use our service.

1. Information We Collect

Account and Sign-In Information

When you sign in using Google, we may receive basic profile information, such as your name and email address, depending on the permissions requested and granted.

Form Creation Instructions

We process the prompts and instructions you submit to generate forms. These may include form titles, descriptions, questions, answer options, and other details you provide.

Google Account Data

If you authorize Google API access, FormPilot AI accesses only the data and functionality allowed by the permissions shown during authorization. These permissions may allow the application to create or manage Google Forms.

Technical Information

Application infrastructure may process technical information, such as browser type, IP address, timestamps, request logs, and error information, for security and troubleshooting. The information actually collected depends on our configuration.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Authenticate users and manage application access.
  • Understand form creation instructions.
  • Create Google Forms using permissions you grant.
  • Display generated forms and related links.
  • Maintain security and troubleshoot technical problems.
  • Respond to support requests and meet legal obligations.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy

FormPilot AI's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements where applicable.

We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. We do not transfer Google user data to third parties for advertising purposes.

Human access to Google user data is restricted to circumstances permitted by Google's policies, such as when the user provides the required consent, when necessary for security, or when required by law.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Information may be processed by our application server, database, hosting provider, or other services necessary to operate FormPilot AI.

The categories of information stored and the retention period depend on the application's actual configuration and the purpose for which the information is needed. We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for service operation, security, and applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, we will delete it or take appropriate steps to remove identifying information, subject to applicable legal requirements.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information or Google user data. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate the application, such as hosting, database, authentication, or AI service providers, where applicable.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security of the service and its users.

6. Third-Party Services

FormPilot AI may use Google Sign-In and Google Forms APIs to provide its features. Third-party services operate under their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices and Google Access

You can stop using FormPilot AI at any time. You can review or revoke the application's access to your Google Account through Google's account connections page.

Manage Google Account Connections

Revoking access may disable features that depend on Google APIs. It does not automatically delete information already stored by FormPilot AI.

9. Data Deletion Requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your use of FormPilot AI, subject to applicable law.

Send a request to our contact email with the subject "FormPilot AI - Data Deletion Request". Do not include passwords or Google access tokens in your request.

We will review requests and respond within a reasonable period. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or necessary for legitimate security purposes.

10. Children's Privacy

FormPilot AI is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided information inappropriately, contact us so that we can review the matter.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our application, data practices, or legal obligations change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
